The Iraq Quagmire Dialogue *

Right hand, holding a gun: "Left hand, I'm going to shoot us in the foot."

Left hand: "What? Don't do that! It'll bleed! It'll hurt!"

Right hand shoots foot.

Right hand: "Holy shit. Our foot's bleeding."

Left hand: "What the fuck! You just shot us in our foot! Are you fucking out of your mind?"

Right hand: "Why do you bitch all the time? Okay, fine. You're in charge now. What's your great idea?"

Left hand: "What the fuck do you mean? My idea was not shooting us in the foot."

Right hand: "So you don't have a Plan B?"

Left hand: "A Plan B? A plan other than not shooting ourselves in the foot? How about this: don't shoot us in the fucking foot?"

Right hand: "So you have no Plan B. So really, you just hate our foot, which you demonstrate by taking joy from the fact that your prediction that the foot would be bleed happened to be correct, though the evidence is still ambiguous. Do you really enjoy being right more than you regret the fact that our foot is bleeding?"

Left hand throws itself into the air in exasperation.
