We can do better
Let me live long enough to go out in "the big one".
- - - - - - - - - -
by Peter Jensen

"We can do better. You have the power."

- Howard Dean

HIRTY YEARS AGO we experienced a gasoline crisis - I am sure that everyone remembers the long lines and huge increase in price then. The future stared us in the face - oil is finite and the vast majority of it lay under the sands of a politically volatile region a world away.

A consciousness, an awareness was then born. The environmental movement began, recycling was the thing to do, solar power was the hope. Since then we have descended into a material hedonism that has gives us the trophy home (mortgaged to the hilt) and the Hummer (financed at 0%). The war in Iraq is only a politico-militaristic manifestation of that attitude, a response to our irresponsibe leadership over the years and a corruption of the moral fortitude of the public assuaged by ever increasing, yet useless material comforts and mind numbing, soul deadening mass entertainment.

Our politial misery reflects a subtle, yet pervasive erosion of democracy from the power once held in the hands of the people to a stranglehold by the corporation. It alone controls the levers of economic and political power. Jobs are lost to chase low wages around the world, first manufacturing and now the beginning of the overseas outsourcing of high level service employment. Consumerism is driven by ever higher levels of credit as income levels plummet. Dying monopolies (the oil industry) protect their last gasp for profit at the cost of American lives.

Iraq - it was no mistake at all, it is a logical outcome of where we are as a society and culture. Certainly this Administration used trumped up charges and hyperbolic rhetoric to persuade the people to support the effort. Peace activist, chanting "No Blood for Oil", may have thought they identified the underlying reasons for the action but the corporate domination of the oil industry is a veneer - western oil companies control the oil markets regardless of who is in power, wherever.

The real agenda is hidden - the protection of the American Dollar that is used to pay for the oil on the world market (Iraq was the first oil producing country to demand Euros instead of Dollars as payment for their oil). An erosion of the value of the Dollar spells doom to the facade of a high standard of living in this country. And with that, the middle class of this country would finally collide with the ceiling of upward mobility and recognize their downward spiral into poverty. The Bush tax cuts accelerates the ever increasing pressure on wage earners to pay more and more and allows capital to accumulate without constraint.

When hope of a brighter future is dimmed, then rage will erupt - even if it is only over the inability to purchase the next best thing. Then woe to the traitors of the American dream. Those in power know it - that is why this scenario is played out over long periods of time and under cover of "compassionate" leadership.

So what does this all mean. Energy, jobs, corporate power, monetary issues - they are are intertwined and all one and the same - the rise of the oligarchy and the fascist control of its power. This tendency is evident everywhere, from the rise of the political-military-industrial complex (yes, that was Eisenhower's first choice in defining the phenomenon, but he was persuaded to shorten it to a less drastic, but much more revealing, description) to the erosion of the Republic by the central Federal authority to the seemingly apathetic response of the people as they struggle for their daily existance.

What can be done? Political leadership derived from the people for the people is the only peaceful way to proceed.

The real hope of the Dean campaign is this reaching out to the people and the delivery of the message - "We can do Better" and his urgent claim "You have the Power". Continue to build on this momentum and drive even further towards an inclusiveness that is still lacking in the campaign. White middle/upper class computer nerds do not elect presidents - the working Joes throughout the country are the ones that do. But they will only be on board the Dean campaign if they understand that their future is at stake.

Define a domestic agenda for Jobs and Energy and Money that rekindles the American dream and the spirit that made this country great. We must go into the inevitable future with hope and not despair.

Define an exit strategy in Iraq now, one that does not involve more troops but rather empowers the Iraqi people to build their own destiny. While this may be national suicide and risky for American domination of the region, especially with our current dependance on foreign oil, it is the right and moral course of action.

The Islamic world is of the mindset that their rulers want capitalism without democracy and their people want democracy without capitalism. This is a dynamic we Americans, who have so ignorantly perverted the Jeffersonian concept of "the pursuit of happiness", cannot understand, let alone resolve. We can only let the Islamic world do it themselves.

"We can do Better.You have the Power."

"We or You" - inclusive // "can do or have the" - empowering // "better or power" - positive and hopeful.

This is the only course. Dean has started on the right path. Let us help him widen that path and continue down it.
