Dubbing Dubya
Handing out nicknames to pols and journalists is one of the one endearing trait of our feckless leader. But the names we've heard about aren't the ones used in private.
- - - - - - - - - -
skreed.com


F THERE IS ONE CULTURAL CONTRIBUTION to result from the advent of the Bush administration over these last two glorious years, it may be the proliferation of fun and clever nicknames flying back and forth across the nation's capital.

Naturally perhaps, it is the President (or Resident) himself that has garnered more terms of endearment from the media, particularly the independent media, than anyone else. Among the more ubiquitous of them... Smirk, Shrub, Bush Lite, Boy George, Chimp in Chief, Incurious George (see sidebar), and Commander in Thief.

It is a well publicized fact that George W. has a penchant for bestowing special names on certain significant personalities both within the beltway and beyond. For example, Kenneth Lay was called "Kenny Boy", Rep. Barney Frank is "Saber Tooth", former President Bush is "41", Defense Sec. Donald Rumsfeld is known as "Rummy", Karl Rove is the "Boy Genius", Barbara Bush = "No. 1", just to name a few. To be christened in this way by the world's most powerful man is understandably considered an honor of sorts.
