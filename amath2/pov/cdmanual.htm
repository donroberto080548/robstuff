Getting Started

To install PoV under Microsoft Windows 95/98/ME/NT/2000

1. Insert the PoV CD 1 in your CD-ROM drive.
2. Click Start.
3. Click Run.
4. Type D:\SETUP, and then press ENTER.
 Note D: designates the drive letter of your CD-ROM drive.

Starting PoV
During installation, PoV creates its own Program group on your Start menu.

1. Click Start (or click the PoV icon on your desktop).
2. Point to Programs.
3. Point to Point of View group and then click the Point of View icon.

To uninstall PoV
Repeat the install procedure described above. You will be presented with a list of options including the option to Remove the program from your computer.

Main Menu
The first screen to appear is the Main menu. There are a variety of options to choose from, but to begin your interactive adventure, choose Play Movie. The next screen will be the Play Movie options screen. The first time you start, you will choose the Start from beginning option. The movie will begin to play.

Video Control Menu
Moving the mouse while the movie is playing will reveal the Video Control Menu below the video display area. You may click Menu to go directly back to the most recent menu, click Full Screen to resize the video display or Jump Forward to the next video segment within the current Chapter. There are also video control buttons for Search Backward, Search Forward, and Play/Pause.

Note: To use the Search functions, place the pointer over the control, then press and hold down the left mouse button. If no controls are available, it may be because you are in full screen mode. To switch back to regular mode, simply click either mouse button.

Interactive Interludes
PoV is divided into twelve Chapters. It can be played all at once or over multiple sessions. Each movie Chapter ends with an Interactive Interlude. At this point you are asked a few fun, provocative Exit Poll questions. The questions are based upon the character and plot elements as well as on the central themes of the story - fantasy, perception, obsession, fear, and sexuality. These questions are designed to gauge your reactions to the story as it unfolds. The input is used to build a personality profile based on your own ideas, perceptions, predilections, and aversions. As a result, the behavior of the characters and the resulting story itself are modified. There are many alternate scene sequences and three distinctly different endings.

The Interactive Interlude menus have the following options from which to choose:

Option 1: Explore
You get to play the voyeur by snooping into the characters' personal effects, uncovering their personal secrets and gaining insight about their hidden agendas. This supplemental material is rich with relevant, interesting and often humorous content that develops with and enhances the depth of the story.

Option 2: Encounters
Face to face interactions with the characters in which they reveal their innermost thoughts.

Option 3: Review
To view a Chapter again. You may only review Chapters for which you've answered Exit Poll questions. If you press the Menu button while reviewing a Chapter, you will return to the Review menu, and then selecting back will take you to the current Interlude menu.

Option 4: Resume
To continue with the next Chapter of the movie.

Option 5: Quit
PoV is unlike other linear movies. Each viewing is unique and you can't skip ahead in the story. It is very important to quit the movie using this menu option. You will be presented with a screen that will allow you to save your progress to a file.


Additional Notes
PoV is an interactive movie made up of many separate video sequences. As you answer the Exit Poll questions and progress from Chapter to Chapter, the course of the movie will be dynamically created as it plays. Once you get to the end of the movie, your session automatically goes into Your Point of View mode, which allows you to watch the movie you've created from start to finish. If you save the game in this mode, you will be able to watch this unique version of the movie again, non-interactively, at anytime.

There is no "scoring" as in games, and a viewer cannot make an incorrect response. There are no "cheats" or pre-set paths for viewing all the alternate scenes and multiple endings. Playing PoV is not a goal-oriented activity. The makers of the movie hope that you watch it at least once and find that version entertaining. If you someday watch it again, it is hoped that you are entertained by that version as well.
