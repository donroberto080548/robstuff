What is Social Network Marketing?

Social Network Marketing (SNM) is the use of social networks, online communities, blogs, wikis or any other online collaborative media for marketing, sales, public relations and customer service.

At Landeros Design, it is a process undertaken after careful assessment and strategic planning with our clients. Our marketing experts will undertake the design and implementation of SNM strategies and programs for you, and will either port those programs to a client-managed environment or assign them to our in-house campaign management team to ensure sustainability.

Entry level SNM programs include a strategically planned, coordinated presence on four major sites:

  • FaceBook
  • Twitter
  • YouTube
  • Flickr

After the initial period of discovery, there is assessment and inventory building that takes place - concurrent with the strategic planning process. All you need do is provide your digital and marketing assets to our marketing professionals. Such assets would include videos, photographic images, graphics/corporate ID, audio (speeches & presentations), biographies, backgrounders, brochures and the like.

We will take it from there, providing guidance, lists, questionnaires and templates to assist in first-stage processes.
