Guns, death, destruction, sleaze...If you think that’s all there is to video games then you don’t know Diddly. Let’s Do Diddly is great entertainment.  If you like trivia, crosswords and laughing out loud then you’ll love Diddly, a clever, challenging game from designers Peter Oliphant and Rob Landeros, whose games have collectively sold over $200 million.

Let's Do Diddly is a game for two or three players that combines the fun and excitement of such classic TV and board games as Wheel of Fortune, Password and Trivial Pursuit with elements of Battleship, Concentration and Scrabble. There is even a touch of the New York Times Crossword Puzzle.

Sound complex? It isn’t. It’s simple, fast and lots of fun. It’s the Ultimate Gameshow.

Come on, don’t be scared!  You can handle it!  It’s fun! And funny! With 120 puzzles plus 60 bonus rounds, you’ll get hours – no weeks – maybe even years – of entertainment from this one source. Total playing time for one "show" is about 40 minutes. For those with less time on their hands, there is an option to play a shorter round.

To see what the game looks like, visit our Screen Shot Gallery. For details on how the game is played, see our How to Play page. But best of all, you can watch the game in action on YouTube.
